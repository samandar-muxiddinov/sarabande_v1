# SARabande — NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 · Dancing with the SARs

Did your field get water this season? SARabande answers this question for every field in the Qarshi Steppe, Uzbekistan, from NASA–ISRO NISAR L-band radar (L2 GCOV, HH and HV, 22 June – 1 November 2026, four orbits).

**Open the platform:** this repository is published with GitHub Pages — open `index.html` (the Pages link of this repository). It runs entirely in the browser; no login, no server.

- Simple view: find a field by GPS, coordinates or the map; see how many times it was watered, when, how it compares with the neighbours, and what to do next.
- For water users' associations: squares with no water while their neighbours were watered.
- Expert mode: map explorer, field reports, four-orbit fusion, validation against NASA HLS and NASA POWER, method and open data.

Data: NASA–ISRO NISAR L2 GCOV (ASF DAAC), NASA HLS, NASA POWER. Town positions: GeoNames (CC BY 4.0). Globe outlines: Natural Earth (public domain). Fonts: Unbounded, Manrope, JetBrains Mono (SIL OFL).
